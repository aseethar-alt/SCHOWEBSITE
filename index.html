What We Do

Three ways we take action

Founded in Fall 2019 at USC, SCHO partners with churches, elementary schools, food banks, FQHCs, and community organizations across Los Angeles to bridge the health insurance literacy gap.

Community Outreach

We partner with local organizations to educate LA residents on health insurance, and serve as a resource for USC students navigating the Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP).

Resources & Education

We create informative flyers, infographics, and videos on Medicare, Medi-Cal, and Covered California — all available in English and Spanish to ensure accessibility for all.

Professional Development

We host guest speaker events and seminars each semester, giving members the chance to explore healthcare research trends and policy news from field experts.

Partnering with Covered California & the Riverside Black Chamber of Commerce

This past semester, we partnered with the Riverside Black Chamber of Commerce and Covered California to hold an information booth about healthcare at USC. Our Mission

At SCHO, we believe health insurance information should be understandable and readily accessible to all. By providing free-of-cost educational materials and informational sessions to underinsured populations, we aim to demystify the enrollment process, help individuals and families navigate the healthcare system, and expand coverage in Los Angeles and beyond.

Get Enrolled

Three easy steps to coverage

SCHO's Certified Enrollment Counselors offer free appointments — by phone or Zoom — to help you enroll directly through Covered California.

1

Sign up for a time

Request an appointment below and we'll find a time that works for you.

2

Gather your documents

Have the items below ready. Everything is kept private by law — applying has no effect on immigration status or citizenship.

  • Social Security Number
  • Proof of identity
  • Citizenship status
  • Proof of income
  • Coverage info
3

Get enrolled

We'll reach out by phone (or Zoom, on request) at your scheduled appointment time to complete enrollment together.
